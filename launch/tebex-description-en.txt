Staff Integrity Suite Core

Reports, moderation from the ticket, duty tracking and a record of what your staff does that can't be edited without it showing.

Every action goes into a SHA-256 chain: each entry stores the hash of the previous one. If someone edits, deletes or reorders a record, verification tells you which entry broke. Every 20 entries the hash is copied to your Discord and to a separate file.

What's included:
- Tickets with claim, transfer, priorities, internal notes, canned replies and a 1-5 player rating.
- Evidence with screenshot-basic: the capture is recorded in the chain and verified when you open it.
- Moderation from the ticket: goto, bring, freeze, heal, kick, warn, ban, spectate and revive. Temporary or permanent bans that cover all of the player's accounts.
- Nobody can sanction a higher rank. Bans and warnings need a reason.
- Duty and per-staff stats: resolved tickets, response time, rating and hours.
- Logs ESX, QBCore, Qbox and txAdmin events.
- Standalone, ESX, QBCore or Qbox. JSON with no database, or oxmysql with automatic migrations.
- Spanish and English. Config, locales and SQL are open; the rest uses Cfx Asset Escrow.

It doesn't replace txAdmin and it isn't an anticheat: it keeps what your staff does in plain sight.
